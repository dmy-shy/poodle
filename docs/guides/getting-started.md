# Getting started with Poodle

1. Open the app supplied by your institution.
2. Sign in with your existing account or follow your invitation to verify your email and set a password.
3. Open **My courses** to find your course materials, assignments, and grades. Available actions depend on your course role.
4. Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen if you need to reset your password.

For a local demonstration with fixtures, see the [Flutter README](../../poodle_flutter/README.md).
